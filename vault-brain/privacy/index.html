vault-brain privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

vault-brain is a personal tool used only by its developer, Hilton Meyer, with his own Google account.

What it accesses

The app requests the Gmail read-only scope (gmail.readonly) to read email in the developer's own mailbox. It cannot send, modify or delete email.

How data is used and stored

Email content is copied into a SQLite database stored locally on the developer's own computer. It is used only to provide search over the developer's own information. Data is not uploaded to any server operated by the app.

Sharing

Data is not sold, shared with, or transferred to third parties, and is not used for advertising or to train AI models. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the local database removes all stored data.
